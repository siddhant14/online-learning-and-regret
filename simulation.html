WHAT IS MEASURED

In Hedge mode, the learner’s loss is the average expert loss weighted by the current probabilities. This is an expected loss; the simulation doesn’t sample an expert.

COMPARATOR & FEEDBACK

The benchmark is the best single expert over the run so far. Every expert’s loss is revealed after each round. Regret can go down between rounds. With equal starting weights and one fixed learning rate, Hedge’s cumulative mixture regret stays nonnegative. Deterministic Weighted Majority can have negative regret.
